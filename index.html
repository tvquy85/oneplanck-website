Research

Peer-reviewed foundations, translated into systems.

Our research program spans machine learning for financial forecasting, reinforcement learning, robust evaluation, agentic AI, and quantitative decision systems.

Financial ML

Forecasting under changing market regimes

We study how predictive systems behave when market conditions, data-generating processes, and economic constraints shift over time.

Reinforcement Learning

Learning systems for sequential financial decisions

We investigate learning and evaluation methods for sequential decision-making where transaction costs, risk, and regime dependence matter.

Evaluation

Robust claims, not headline metrics

Our work emphasizes reproducibility, claim auditing, stress testing, and decision-relevant evaluation rather than single benchmark numbers.

Agentic AI

AI systems for scientific research workflows

We explore how frontier language models can support research planning, evidence synthesis, coding, experiment analysis, and technical reporting while keeping human researchers in control.

About OnePlanck

Research discipline, product ambition.

OnePlanck is an AI research and technology company focused on quantitative finance. We combine machine learning, quantitative methods, financial data, and agentic AI to develop rigorous research and decision-support systems.

Our approach is grounded in peer-reviewed research and a simple principle: financial AI should be evaluated under the conditions in which real decisions are made.
